What Debato is

A poll is a snapshot. A debate is an argument.

Most apps let you tap a option and move on. Debato keeps the question open for as long as you set it, shows the split as it shifts, and gives people room to say why they voted the way they did.

📊

Live results

Percentages and vote counts update while the debate runs, so you watch opinion move rather than reading a final number.

⏱️

Debates that close

Every debate has a countdown. When it ends the result is locked in and a winner is revealed — no threads that drift on forever.

💬

Comments and replies

Argue your side in threaded comments, reply to other people, and react with emoji when a point lands.

🖼️

Add a picture

Attach a photo to any debate — the match, the poster, the thing being rated. Images are resized on your phone before upload.

🔔

Know when it matters

Get notified when your debate is approved, when someone replies to you, and when a debate you voted in finishes.

🛡️

Reviewed before it is live

Debates submitted by users are checked by a moderator first, pictures included, so the feed stays worth reading.

How it works

Three steps

Ask something

Write your question, choose a format, set how long it should run and add a picture if it helps.

People vote

Your debate goes to the feed once a moderator approves it. Votes and comments come in while the clock runs.

See the verdict

When the time is up the debate closes, the final split is locked in and the winning side is revealed.

Real people, reviewed debates

Taking part needs a verified email address, so a throwaway account cannot vote or comment. Debates and pictures submitted by users are reviewed by a moderator before they reach anyone's feed, and anything that breaks the rules can be removed.
