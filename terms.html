4. Content rules

Debato exists for disagreement, and a strongly argued opinion is welcome. These rules are about conduct, not about which side you take.

Debate topics

  • Write a clear question that can actually be debated. Do not use the topic field for advertising, spam or a link dump.
  • Choose options that fairly represent the positions. Do not write loaded options designed so only one answer is possible.
  • Set a duration that suits the question rather than one designed to game the feed.

Images

  • Only upload an image that relates to the debate.
  • Only upload images you own or are allowed to use. Do not upload film posters, album art, stock photography, logos or other material belonging to someone else without permission.
  • Do not upload images of identifiable people who have not agreed to appear, and never images of children in any sexual, suggestive or exploitative context.

Comments and replies

  • Argue with the point, not the person.
  • Do not post other people's personal information — addresses, phone numbers, workplaces, private messages or identity documents.
  • Do not post the same comment repeatedly or flood a debate.
